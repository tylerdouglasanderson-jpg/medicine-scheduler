1What this tool does

This app builds an optimal monthly inpatient-medicine call schedule using an exact solver — not a heuristic, not a greedy fill. It runs entirely in your browser: nothing you type is uploaded anywhere (unless you choose to send a problem report, see §13), and once the page is open it keeps working with no internet connection at all. When you're happy with a month, you export a colored spreadsheet that looks exactly like what's on your screen, ready to print or hand to the team.

  1. 1 Set up — team, month, roster, commitments.
  2. 2 Solve — one button, up to five different schedules.
  3. 3 Compare & iterate — flip between solutions, pin a cell, freeze a date, re-solve.
  4. 4 Export — styled xlsx or print to PDF.

2The 6-day call cycle

Everything the solver does is built around one repeating six-day pattern. Understanding it makes every other panel in this app make sense — read this section even if you skip the rest.

  1. 1 Pre-call
  2. 2 Call
  3. 3 Post-call
  4. 4 Post-post-call (PPC)
  5. 5 Short Call 1 (SC1)
  6. 6 Short Call 2 (SC2) — then the cycle repeats.

Call day

The whole on-service team works. Exactly one person takes night call — an intern, when two or more interns are on service; a team senior instead, for Medicine C teams with no interns on. With two seniors and one intern all month, the intern takes every other night (never two in a row) and the two seniors split the rest as evenly as possible — one night each in most months; in a month with six call days one senior takes a second night. Everyone else takes day call. No one is "off" on a call day, and there is no pager holder on call days — the whole team is present, so the pager doesn't need a dedicated owner.

Post-call

The person who took night call is the sleeper: they round in the morning, then leave. That rest does not count against their off quota — it's compensation for the night, not a vacation day. The post-call pager goes to the prior day's day-call intern; if there wasn't a day-call intern that day, a working senior holds it instead.

Good to know

A pre-call day that lands on a Tuesday or Thursday is a morning-report day.

Get this right first

The anchor type in Setup tells the app which of these six phases the 1st of the month is, so it can lay the cycle across the whole calendar. Set it wrong and every call day in the month shifts.

3Setup panel — every field

FieldWhat it is & why it matters
Team (A–F) Which inpatient team you're scheduling. Medicine C carries a special senior-only call rule: a team senior can take night call when no interns are on that service.
Month The calendar month. This drives the entire grid — set it first, before anything else.
Anchor type The cycle phase (see §2) that day 1 of the month falls on. If the month starts post-call, you must also fill in carry-in below.
Off quota The number of counted days off for a resident on service the full month. The app pro-rates this for each resident by their actual service days (rounded half up), so a half-month seat gets half. Typical value: 4 — so 4 in a full month, 2 on a two-week window. This is a hard line: the solver gives everyone their full number or reports the month as infeasible. It will never quietly hand someone a day less.
Attempt golden weekend A soft goal: give each resident a full Saturday+Sunday off together when the schedule can afford it. Never overrides a hard rule.
Allow seniors off on short-call days Off by default. Normally the solver steers seniors away from a day off on a weekday short-call day, because short call takes admissions and the senior is wanted for them. Tick this for a month when the interns admit on their own; senior days off can then land on any short-call day. Weekend short call takes no admissions, so it is never protected either way.
Have a senior present on the 1st On by default: a soft goal that keeps seniors from being off on the first day of the month, when the team changes over. Untick it to let a senior's day off fall there.
Carry-in Appears only when the anchor is post-call. Records who was night, day-call intern, and day-call senior on the last call day of the previous month, so this month's cycle continues correctly from where the last one left off.

4Roster panel

ColumnWhat it is & why it matters
Name The resident's identifier on the grid.
Role Intern or senior — this drives call composition and who is eligible to take night call.
Kind Training background: Family, Transitional, Psych, OB/GYN, or Other. Together with Role it sets each resident's default didactics day (see below).
Start / End The service window — the dates this resident is actually on the team. A resident leaving mid-month gets a shorter window and an automatically pro-rated off quota.
Didactics: day / half / hard The weekly teaching half-day. It's filled in automatically from Role and Kind — seniors and Psych interns Tue PM, Transitional interns Wed PM, Family interns Thu PM — and you can change the day, half, or protection for any resident. Marking it hard makes it protected: the solver only puts them on the pager over hard didactics when there is truly no other option, and it raises a warning when it does.

Teaching time is protected for everyone, interns first. The solver will not park a day off on someone's own didactics day, and it keeps interns off the pager on their own didactics afternoon wherever it can — an intern carrying the pager through didactics is barely there at all, and carrying it is normally a senior's job. Nobody is listed for didactics on a call or post-call day: those are not sessions anyone could have made, so they never count against the total.

Add resident and Remove do what they say.

Split seat

The mid-month handoff helper. Use it when one service line needs to be covered by two different people during the month — it duplicates a seat with a replacement resident whose service starts the day after the original resident's ends, and runs through month-end. After splitting, adjust the replacement's dates and didactics to match the real handoff.

5Commitments · PTO · Pins

This panel is a chip editor: choose a mode, then click days directly on a resident's month strip to toggle that mark on or off. Click a chip's × to remove it.

Commitments

Commitment (AM) and Commitment (PM) mark a recurring half-day obligation — clinic, a procedure block, admin time. You'll be prompted for a label. The solver keeps the resident working around it, but a commitment that lands on a call or post-call day is a hard error — fix it with your program admin, since call and post-call can't be split around an outside obligation.

PTO

Marks a vacation or leave day. Like commitments, PTO on a call or post-call day is a hard error — move the PTO to a day that isn't call-adjacent.

The 7 pin types

Pins are your manual override: force a specific assignment for a specific person on a specific day.

PinForces
Off (counted)A day off that counts toward the resident's quota.
Off (free / bonus)An extra day off that does not count against quota — shown as "bonus".
Must workThe resident is working that day.
PagerThe resident holds the pager that day.
Day callThe resident works the call day as day-call, not night.
Night callThe resident is the night-call person that day.
Half off (AM) / Half off (PM)A half day off — an extra freebie: it never counts toward anyone's days off, however many they get (two halves are not a day off). Shown as "½ off" on rounders and counted under Perks.
Shortcut

You don't have to come back to this panel for every pin — clicking a solved calendar cell creates pins too (see §7).

6Solve

Press Solve to build the optimal schedule for the month. The button is disabled while there are unresolved hard errors — fix those first (see §11). While it runs you'll see a brief "Solving…" state.

No feasible schedule

If the constraints as entered can't be satisfied by any schedule, you'll get a plain-language diagnosis naming the likely culprit pins or constraints. Relax a pin or fix the roster, then re-solve.

A good solve builds up to five different schedules (see §8). After a successful solve you get the full calendar, the totals table, and Potential Issues — soft warnings from an independent auditor that double-checks the solver's work from scratch (see §12).

7Iterating: pins + freeze-through-date

This is the heart of daily use. A first solve is rarely your last — this is how you shape it into the schedule you'll actually ship.

Click a solved cell

Click any OFF, PAGER, or ROUNDERS cell in the solved calendar and a small popover lets you pin that person to an assignment (or clear an existing pin there), then re-solve immediately.

Freeze through

The date box next to Solve locks every assignment on or before that date to the current solution, then re-solves only the remaining days. Use it once the first week or two look right, so tweaking later weeks doesn't reshuffle work you've already approved. Clear the date to free the whole month again.

The loop

Solve → eyeball → freeze the part that's right → pin a fix → re-solve → repeat.

8Trying different versions

Every Solve builds up to five different schedules, all of which follow every rule. They appear as tabs above the calendar: Solution 1 to Solution 5. Click a tab to switch the whole screen — calendar, totals, Potential Issues, and exports — to that schedule.

  • Solution 1 is your current schedule: the best fit for your inputs, changed as little as possible from what you had on screen.
  • Solutions 2–5 are real alternatives. Each has a short note under the tabs saying how it differs from Solution 1 — whose days off moved, who picked up different nights, how the pager splits, and whether it is just as balanced.
  • They are meaningfully different on days off, and never noticeably worse. If fewer than five truly different schedules exist (for example most days off are pinned, frozen, or blocked by clinic or PTO), you get fewer and the app tells you why.

The tab you pick becomes the schedule the app builds on: pinning a cell, using Freeze through, or pressing Solve again rebuilds all the tabs around that choice. Changing the setup, roster, commitments or PTO clears the tabs (they would no longer be valid).

Keep them, reopen them, share them

Save my month (.json) keeps every solution, and which tab you were on, in the file. Reopen it later with Open a saved month…, or send it to a co-chief — they see the same tabs, with the same one selected.

9Reading the calendar, legend, and totals

The calendar lays out in Sun–Sat week blocks. Each day has rows for DATE/TYPE (the cycle phase — CALL is highlighted), ROUNDERS (the day team — call days show "Day - X" / "Night - Y", post-call days are tagged "(postcall)", half-off shows "X - ½ off"), PAGER, CLINIC, DIDACTICS, PTO, and OFF.

Morning Report days

Any pre-call day that falls on a Tuesday or Thursday is a Morning Report day — your team presents. Those TYPE cells are outlined in purple and tagged MORNING REPORT on screen, in the exported xlsx, and in print, so the team can see at a glance which mornings are theirs.

The solver also protects the coverage on those days: it keeps at least two people on, and prefers a senior and an intern over any other pairing. Two interns is acceptable; one person alone is a last resort. These are strong preferences, not hard rules — a day where nothing else works can still go thin, and you'll see W_MR_THIN, W_MR_NO_SENIOR, or W_MR_NO_INTERN in Potential Issues when it does.

Which days become days off

Pre-call is the lightest day of the cycle and short-call 2 is next, so the solver prefers to place days off there — a pre-call day is the natural one to run with one fewer person. That preference is deliberately weaker than the rule spacing days off across the weeks, so it won't bunch two of someone's days off into the same week just to land on a pre-call. Morning Report pre-calls are excluded from the preference.

Seniors are steered away from days off on weekday short-call days (short call takes admissions). Weekend short call takes none, so a senior off on a Saturday or Sunday short-call day costs nothing — and the Allow seniors off on short-call days setting lifts the weekday preference too.

On a two-person team (one intern, one senior) a single resident runs the day whenever the other is off, so days off work the same way as on a bigger team. Every other team keeps at least two people on the day team.

10Exporting & sharing

Personal calendars

Choose a resident and click Download calendar for a personal .ics file, or choose All residents (.zip) for one calendar per person in a single ZIP. Import the files into Google Calendar, Apple Calendar, or Outlook. Each calendar includes rounding/call, pager duty, clinic and other obligations, didactics, Morning Report, post-call, OFF, and PTO in Central Time.

Spreadsheet

Export spreadsheet downloads a formatted .xlsx whose colors match this grid, with readable widths, wrapped rows, and frozen headers for Excel or Google Sheets. Download & open Google Sheets downloads the same workbook and opens a new Sheet; in Sheets, use File → Import → Upload to select the download.

One solution or all of them

When there is more than one solution, a selector next to the export buttons lets you choose This solution (the tab on screen — exactly the exports described above) or All solutions:

  • Spreadsheet — one workbook with a sheet per solution ("Solution 1", "Solution 2", …). Sheets 2 and up start with the short note on how they differ from Solution 1. Download & open Google Sheets uses the same workbook.
  • Calendars — one ZIP with a folder per solution ("Solution 1/", "Solution 2/", …). If you picked a single resident, each folder holds just their file.

Print

Browser print (→ Save as PDF) of the solution on screen: its calendar, totals and its "how it differs" note. The setup controls and the tabs are hidden automatically.

Save / Open a saved month

Under Your work at the bottom of the screen, Save my month (.json) downloads the whole month — team, roster, commitments, PTO, pins, and all your solutions — as a .json file. Open a saved month… reads one back. This is how you carry work between sessions, keep a reusable team template, or share a month with a co-chief: send them the file, they open it and see the same tabs.

Autosave

The app also autosaves to the browser automatically. The JSON file is your durable, portable copy — the one that survives a new machine or a cleared browser.

Clear scenario

Wipes everything — setup, roster, commitments, PTO, pins, and the solved schedule — back to an empty app, after a confirmation prompt. Use it to start a fresh month from scratch. It also clears the autosave, so Save my month first if you might want this month back.

11Errors glossary — hard, block Solve

CodeWhat it meansHow to fix
COMMITMENT_ON_CALL A commitment or clinic half-day lands on a call or post-call day. Move the commitment, or clear it with your program admin.
PTO_ON_CALL PTO lands on a call or post-call day. Move the PTO to a non-call-adjacent day.
PIN_OUTSIDE_WINDOW A pin targets someone outside their service window. Remove or fix the pin, or correct their start/end dates.
NIGHT_PIN_PM_COMMITMENT Someone is pinned to night call but also has a PM commitment that day. Remove one of the two conflicting marks.
CONTRADICTORY_PINS Two pins conflict — e.g. off + must-work the same day, two people both pinned night or pager the same day, an off pin on a call/post-call day, or a day/night pin on a non-call day. Remove one of the conflicting pins.
CARRYIN_REQUIRED The anchor is post-call but carry-in wasn't filled in. Fill in carry-in (see §3).
DEGENERATE_COMPOSITION A call day's on-service roster can't staff call — e.g. no senior on that day, or fewer than two people. Adjust service windows or the roster.
QUOTA_IMPOSSIBLE Someone has fewer eligible days than their off quota — every other day is call, post-call, PTO, or a commitment. Days off are a hard requirement, so this can never be solved around. Free up a day (move a clinic), or lower the off quota.

After an app update

If the app has been updated since you last solved a month, opening that scenario shows a note above the calendar: "This schedule was built under older scheduling rules." Press Solve and it rebuilds the month under the current rules — your roster, clinics, PTO, pins and attending days are all kept. You do not need to clear the scenario or re-enter anything. A schedule solved by the current version keeps re-solving stably, exactly as before.

If you ever see this

The app also folds any independent-audit hard-rule violation into this same panel. If you ever see a code starting with A_ here, the solver and the auditor disagree with each other — treat it as a bug and report it.

13Report a problem

If a schedule looks wrong, the app says something confusing, or you have an idea, press the amber Report a problem button at the top of the screen. You can also use the Send this month to us links that appear next to a "No feasible schedule" message, under Potential Issues, and when the checker finds a broken rule.

What gets sent — and only this, only when you press Send:

  • your message,
  • the app version, and
  • your month (setup, roster, pins and your solutions) if the box "Attach my schedule (recommended)" is ticked. It is ticked by default because it lets us see exactly what you see. Untick it to send just your message.

Nothing else is sent anywhere, and nothing is sent unless you press Send.

14Get the app

The Download button at the top gets you the newest version so you can run it offline on your own computer — no install, no account, nothing uploaded. The downloaded file sends nothing anywhere unless you use Report a problem. (The website itself counts anonymous page visits; the downloaded file does not.)

  • Download the app (one HTML file) — the whole app as a single file. Save it anywhere and double-click to open it in your browser. You always get the newest version.
  • Starter kit (.zip) — the app plus this guide and a few example months, with a one-click "Click me" launcher.

The app never checks for updates on its own. To see whether you are current, compare the version next to the title (click it to see what's new) with the latest release linked from the Download window. If you opened the downloaded file, the window shows which version you have.

16Tips & troubleshooting

  • Set Month and Anchor first — a wrong anchor shifts every call day in the month.
  • Fix all Errors before Solve will do anything.
  • Use Load the example month to see a complete, working month before building your own.
  • Iterate with freeze so approved weeks stay put while you tweak the rest.
  • Colors on screen = colors in the xlsx = colors when you print. One language, everywhere.
  • Everything is local: no data leaves your machine unless you press Send on a problem report. The file from Save my month is your save file.
