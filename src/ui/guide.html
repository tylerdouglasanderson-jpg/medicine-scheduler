1What this tool does

This app builds an optimal monthly inpatient-medicine call schedule using an exact solver — not a heuristic, not a greedy fill. It runs entirely in your browser: nothing you type is uploaded anywhere (unless you choose to send a problem report, see §13), and once the page is open it keeps working with no internet connection at all. When you're happy with a month, you export a colored spreadsheet that looks exactly like what's on your screen, ready to print or hand to the team.

  1. 1 Set up — team, month, roster, commitments.
  2. 2 Solve — one button, up to five different schedules.
  3. 3 Compare & iterate — flip between solutions, pin a cell, freeze a date, re-solve.
  4. 4 Export — styled xlsx or print to PDF.

2The 6-day call cycle

Everything the solver does is built around one repeating six-day pattern. Understanding it makes every other panel in this app make sense — read this section even if you skip the rest.

  1. 1 Pre-call
  2. 2 Call
  3. 3 Post-call
  4. 4 Post-post-call (PPC)
  5. 5 Short Call 1 (SC1)
  6. 6 Short Call 2 (SC2) — then the cycle repeats.

Call day

The whole on-service team works. Exactly one person takes night call — an intern, when two or more interns are on service; a team senior instead, for Medicine C teams with no interns on. With two seniors and one intern all month, the intern takes every other night (never two in a row) and the two seniors split the rest as evenly as possible — one night each in most months; in a month with six call days one senior takes a second night. Everyone else takes day call. No one is "off" on a call day, and there is no pager holder on call days — the whole team is present, so the pager doesn't need a dedicated owner.

Post-call

The person who took night call is the sleeper: they round in the morning, then leave. That rest does not count against their off quota — it's compensation for the night, not a vacation day. The post-call pager goes to the prior day's day-call intern. If that intern has just rotated off the team (a mid-month handoff), the incoming intern holds it; a senior holds it only when no intern can.

Good to know

A pre-call day that lands on a Tuesday or Thursday is a morning-report day.

Get this right first

The anchor type in Setup tells the app which of these six phases the 1st of the month is, so it can lay the cycle across the whole calendar. Set it wrong and every call day in the month shifts.

3Setup panel — every field

FieldWhat it is & why it matters
Team (A–F) Which inpatient team you're scheduling. Medicine C carries a special senior-only call rule: a team senior can take night call when no interns are on that service.
Month The calendar month. This drives the entire grid — set it first, before anything else.
Anchor type The cycle phase (see §2) that day 1 of the month falls on. If the month starts post-call, you must also fill in carry-in below.
Off quota The number of counted days off for a resident on service the full month. The app pro-rates this for each resident by their actual service days (rounded half up), so a half-month seat gets half. Typical value: 4 — so 4 in a full month, 2 on a two-week window. Normally leave it at 4. This is a hard line: the solver gives everyone their full number or reports the month as infeasible. It will never quietly hand someone a day less. The preference Allow more than the quota of days off (below) turns it into a minimum.
Try for a golden weekend A soft goal: give each resident a full Saturday+Sunday off together when the schedule can afford it. Never overrides a hard rule.
Allow seniors off on short-call days Off by default. Normally the solver steers seniors away from a day off on a weekday short-call day, because short call takes admissions and the senior is wanted for them. Tick this for months later in the year, when interns are more independent and you are confident that they can admit on their own; senior days off can then land on any short-call day. Weekend short call still takes no admissions, so it is never protected either way.
Keep a senior present on the 1st On by default: a soft goal that keeps seniors from being off on the first day of the month, when the team changes over. Untick it to let a senior's day off fall there.
Allow more than the quota of days off Not advised. Off by default. Lets the solver give someone more whole days off than the quota, but only as a last resort when the month cannot be staffed any other way (each extra day carries a penalty). It exists for over-scheduled months. For extra time off, add bonus days afterwards instead.
Limit senior pager days to Optional, 0 to 4 (0 means keep seniors off the pager if at all possible). A soft goal: the solver tries to keep each senior from holding the pager more than this many days. If the month cannot meet it, it goes over and keeps the senior's pager days as low as it can, and Potential Issues lists who is over. Leave it on "No limit" to turn it off.
Two-person team Shown only when some call days have exactly one senior and one intern. Solve asks how to run those days, with three choices: the intern takes nights and the senior takes days; both work every day and cross-cover has the nights; or you pin it yourself. The first pins night call to the intern and day call to the senior on each of those days, and allows back-to-back nights for the intern (nothing else in the scheduler allows back-to-back nights). The second schedules both on every day with no night person (cross-cover covers the night and is not scheduled here, so there is no post-call sleeper). Use Choose how… to pick again; your own night and day-call pins on those days are replaced by the first two choices.
Carry-in night person / day-call intern / day-call senior Appear only when the anchor is post-call, and all three are required before you can Solve. They record who took night call, day call (intern) and day call (senior) on the last call day of the previous month, so this month's cycle continues from where the last one left off. (The scheduler does not currently use the day-call senior when building day 1; it is saved with the month.) The Preferences group sits below them.

4Roster panel

ColumnWhat it is & why it matters
Last name The resident's last name, used as their identifier on the grid.
Role Intern or senior — this drives call composition and who is eligible to take night call.
Kind Training background: Family, Transitional, Psych, OB/GYN, or Other. Together with Role it sets each resident's default didactics day (see below).
Start / End The service window — the dates this resident is actually on the team. A resident leaving mid-month gets a shorter window and an automatically pro-rated off quota.
Didactics day / Half / Hard The weekly teaching half-day. It's filled in automatically from Role and Kind — seniors and Psych interns Tue PM, Family interns Thu PM — and you can change the day, half, or protection for any resident. Transitional interns get Wednesday PM selected and greyed out: theirs is every other Wednesday and the starting week changes monthly, so a pop-up asks you to click just the Wednesdays that have it, using the Didactics (PM) mode on their calendar. Those dates count exactly like weekly didactics. Hard is ticked automatically for interns (seniors start soft). Marking it hard makes it protected: the solver only puts them on the pager over hard didactics when there is truly no other option, and it raises a warning when it does.

Teaching time is protected for everyone, interns first. The solver will not park a day off on someone's own didactics day, and it keeps interns off the pager on their own didactics afternoon wherever it can — an intern carrying the pager through didactics is barely there at all, and carrying it is normally a senior's job. Nobody is listed for didactics on a call or post-call day: those are not sessions anyone could have made, so they never count against the total.

Add resident creates an intern of kind Other with no didactics; fill in the name, dates and didactics. Remove deletes the row.

Split seat

The mid-month handoff helper. Use it when one service line needs to be covered by two different people during the month — it duplicates a seat with a replacement resident whose service starts the day after the original resident's ends, and runs through month-end. After splitting, adjust the replacement's dates and didactics to match the real handoff.

5Commitments · PTO · Pins

This panel is a chip editor: choose a mode, then click days directly on a resident's month strip to toggle that mark on or off. Click the same day again to remove its mark.

Commitments

Commitment AM and Commitment PM mark a half-day obligation — clinic, a procedure block, admin time. You'll be prompted for a label; it remembers the last one you typed and starts at "clinic". The solver keeps the resident working around it, but a commitment that lands on a call or post-call day is a hard error — fix it with your program admin, since call and post-call can't be split around an outside obligation.

Didactics (PM) — Transitional interns

Transitional interns have didactics every other Wednesday afternoon, and the starting week changes each month. Pick Didactics (PM), then click only the Wednesdays that have it on that intern's strip. Those sessions count like any didactics (protected from days off and the pager, balanced across the team, shown as attended out of picked). If none are picked you get a warning, which you can ignore if they have none this month.

Force One Person Only / Force Two People Only

Normally at least two people work every day. To make a day run with exactly one person, or exactly two, pick Force One Person Only or Force Two People Only and click that date on any resident's strip. You can keep clicking dates; there is no dialog. The date is listed under Forced Staffing Days. For one person you can name who works alone or leave Solver chooses (it prefers a senior). Everyone else who is free that day gets a bonus day off that does not count against the quota. Click the same mode on a date again to remove it; the other mode switches the count.

A call day cannot be forced (everyone works call), and the day must have room: if more people than the count have a clinic or a Must Work / Pager pin that day, you get an error. On a forced-one day the results show Only X is scheduled on D, as you asked. Older files that used "1 person OK" load as Force One Person Only. Days that already drop to one person on their own (Med C, two-person teams, or heavy PTO) need no marking. Morning Report days keep their thin-staffing warning.

PTO Day

Marks a vacation or leave day. Like commitments, PTO on a call or post-call day is a hard error — move the PTO to a day that isn't call-adjacent.

Pins are your manual override: force a specific assignment for a specific person on a specific day.

Shortcut

You don't have to come back to this panel for every pin — clicking a solved calendar cell creates pins too (see §7).

6Solve

Press Solve to build the optimal schedule for the month. The button is disabled while there are unresolved hard errors — fix those first (see §11). While it runs you'll see a brief "Solving…" state.

No feasible schedule

If the constraints as entered can't be satisfied by any schedule, you'll get a plain-language diagnosis naming the likely culprit pins or constraints. Relax a pin or fix the roster, then re-solve.

A good solve builds up to five different schedules (see §8). After a successful solve you get the full calendar, the totals table, and Potential Issues — soft warnings from an independent auditor that double-checks the solver's work from scratch (see §12).

6aHow it solves — the criteria

A short list, strongest first. The solver adds up penalties and rewards and picks the schedule with the lowest total; the first group can never be broken.

Never broken (hard rules)

  • Exactly one night person per call day; they sleep the next day (no pager, not counted on the team).
  • Everyone on service works the call day. No days off on call or post-call days.
  • Each resident gets at least their quota of whole days off (pro-rated for part-month seats), and exactly the quota unless Allow more than the quota of days off is ticked and the month needs it. Half days, PTO and the bonus days on forced-staffing days never count toward it.
  • No day off on a day with clinic or another commitment.
  • Exactly one pager holder every non-call day: never someone who is off, on PTO, asleep, or in a PM commitment.
  • Post-call pager goes to the previous day-call intern; a senior only if no intern can.
  • Staffing floor: at least 2 working each day (1 for a Med C or 2-person team), unless you force a day to exactly 1 or exactly 2.
  • 2 seniors + 1 intern for the whole month: the intern takes exactly every other call night.
  • Your pins, PTO, service windows and chosen attending-pager days are always honoured.

Almost never broken (very heavy penalties)

  • No back-to-back call nights for the same person.
  • Don't take someone off a protected ("hard") didactics day.
  • The attending covers the pager only as a last resort (unless you chose that date).

Balanced as well as possible (fairness)

  • Shifts worked, as a share of each person's service days — about one shift is worth more than any placement preference below.
  • Interns split night call and day call evenly.
  • Afternoon load (PM clinic + didactics + pager) spread by person; a senior with heavy clinic gets fewer pager days.
  • Missed didactics spread across the team; an intern holding the pager at their own didactics counts against the schedule more than a senior doing it.
  • About one day off per calendar week.

Where days off land (preferences)

  • Keep seniors present on weekday short-call days (SC1 / SC2) and on day 1 of the month. A senior off then is penalised, not forbidden. The "Allow seniors off on short-call days" option lifts the short-call preference; weekend short call is not protected.
  • Offs are pushed toward the lightest days: pre-call first, then SC2 (for seniors, weekday SC1/SC2 are the last choice; see above). Call and post-call days can never be offs.
  • Avoid offs on Morning Report days (Tue/Thu pre-call); keep at least 2 people present, a senior and an intern if possible.
  • Avoid two people off the same day.
  • Optional: reward Sat + Sun both off ("Try for a golden weekend").
  • Re-solving changes as little as possible of the schedule you already have.

7Iterating: pins + freeze-through-date

This is the heart of daily use. A first solve is rarely your last — this is how you shape it into the schedule you'll actually ship.

Click a solved cell

Click any OFF, PAGER, or ROUNDERS cell in the solved calendar and a small popover lets you pin that person to an assignment (or clear an existing pin there), then re-solve immediately.

Freeze through

The date box next to Solve locks every assignment on or before that date to the current solution, then re-solves only the remaining days. Use it once the first week or two look right, so tweaking later weeks doesn't reshuffle work you've already approved. Clear the date to free the whole month again.

The loop

Solve → eyeball → freeze the part that's right → pin a fix → re-solve → repeat.

8Trying different versions

Every Solve builds up to five different schedules, all of which follow every rule. They appear as tabs above the calendar: Solution 1 to Solution 5. Click a tab to switch the whole screen — calendar, totals, Potential Issues, and exports — to that schedule.

  • Solution 1 is your current schedule: the best fit for your inputs, changed as little as possible from what you had on screen.
  • Solutions 2–5 are real alternatives. Each has a short note under the tabs saying how it differs from Solution 1 — whose days off moved, who picked up different nights, how the pager splits, and whether it is just as balanced.
  • They are meaningfully different on days off, and never noticeably worse. If fewer than five truly different schedules exist (for example most days off are pinned, frozen, or blocked by clinic or PTO), you get fewer and the app tells you why.

The tab you pick becomes the schedule the app builds on: pinning a cell, using Freeze through, or pressing Solve again rebuilds all the tabs around that choice. Changing the setup, roster, commitments or PTO clears the tabs (they would no longer be valid).

Under the tabs, Compare solutions opens a table of every solution (quality against Solution 1, Potential Issues, days off moved, nights, weekend days off, pager and afternoons off per person); click a row to select it. Highlight changes outlines the cells that differ from Solution 1.

Wide screens: two-column view

On a screen at least 1100 pixels wide the page opens in a side-by-side view: your inputs on the left and the results on the right, each scrolling on its own. A narrower screen always uses the single-column page. The results panel has a button to switch: Return to single-column view for the normal page, and Return to two-column view to come back (it only appears on a wide screen). While editing, the old schedule stays greyed until you press Solve; Re-solve automatically is on by default and rebuilds it about a second after each change (switch it off if you are making several edits).

Keep them, reopen them, share them

Save my month (.json) keeps every solution, and which tab you were on, in the file. Reopen it later with Open a saved month…, or send it to a co-chief — they see the same tabs, with the same one selected.

9Reading the calendar, legend, and totals

The calendar lays out in Sun–Sat week blocks. Each day has rows for DATE/TYPE (the cycle phase — CALL is highlighted), ROUNDERS (the day team — call days show "Day - X" / "Night - Y", post-call days are tagged "(postcall)", half-off shows "X - 1/2 off"), PAGER, CLINIC, DIDACTICS, PTO, and OFF.

Morning Report days

Any pre-call day that falls on a Tuesday or Thursday is a Morning Report day — your team presents. Those TYPE cells are outlined in purple and tagged MORNING REPORT on screen, in the exported xlsx, and in print, so the team can see at a glance which mornings are theirs.

The solver also protects the coverage on those days: it keeps at least two people on, and prefers a senior and an intern over any other pairing. Two interns is acceptable; one person alone is a last resort. These are strong preferences, not hard rules — a day where nothing else works can still go thin, and you'll see W_MR_THIN, W_MR_NO_SENIOR, or W_MR_NO_INTERN in Potential Issues when it does.

Which days become days off

Pre-call is the lightest day of the cycle and short-call 2 is next, so the solver prefers to place days off there — a pre-call day is the natural one to run with one fewer person. That preference is deliberately weaker than the rule spacing days off across the weeks, so it won't bunch two of someone's days off into the same week just to land on a pre-call. Morning Report pre-calls are excluded from the preference.

Seniors are steered away from days off on weekday short-call days (short call takes admissions). Weekend short call takes none, so a senior off on a Saturday or Sunday short-call day costs nothing — and the Allow seniors off on short-call days setting lifts the weekday preference too.

On a two-person team (one intern, one senior) a single resident runs the day whenever the other is off, so days off work the same way as on a bigger team. Every other team keeps at least two people on the day team.

10Exporting & sharing

The Export menu: what each button makes

The Export menu always exports one solution at a time. By default that is the solution on screen. If there is more than one, Which solution lets you pick Solution 1 to 5 (the one on screen is marked), and the buttons use that pick. There is no export-all.

  • Download calendar (.ics) makes calendar files to import into Outlook, Google Calendar or Apple Calendar. It is not the spreadsheet. Whose calendar (.ics file) picks one resident or Everyone (ZIP of .ics files), which is one .ics per person in a ZIP.
  • Export Excel spreadsheet (.xlsx) makes the Excel file to work from through the month, or to send to the chief for approval.
  • Print / Save as PDF makes a PDF of the solution on screen (see Print below).

Personal calendars

Each calendar includes rounding/call, pager duty, clinic and other obligations, didactics, Morning Report, post-call, OFF, and PTO in Central Time.

Spreadsheet

The .xlsx has colors that match this grid, readable widths, wrapped rows, and frozen headers for Excel or Google Sheets. Download & open Google Sheets first explains the steps, then downloads the same workbook and opens a blank Sheet; in Sheets, use File → Open → Upload and pick the file from your Downloads folder. Every day off on a Forced Staffing Day is written as "(bonus)".

Bonus days given

Under the totals is a grid with one row per day and three boxes per resident: AM and PM (a half day off) and Day (a whole bonus day off). Put an X in a box and that resident's totals update on their own: a Day adds 1 to Days and to Off + Bonus and takes 1 off Shifts; an AM or PM adds 1 to Perks (half days) and takes ½ off Shifts. Days and Perks (half days) sit under one Bonus/Off header; Off + Bonus is every day off: Off + Days + half of Perks (a half day counts as ½). Every total in the box is a live formula over the calendar, so editing a name in the calendar (say, deleting an OFF cell) moves the totals too. The grid starts empty — bonus and half days you pinned before solving are already written into the calendar and counted from there. PM off is read from the calendar text, so it can differ slightly from the on-screen number (it cannot see a morning-only commitment). Didactics day (the column to the right) is an input: change it if a resident's didactics weekday changes.

  • The Already off / PTO column lists who is off that day. A box turns red if you tick someone who is already off, or tick both a half and the whole day.
  • Grey boxes are days that resident isn't on the team.
  • The boxes start empty. Type an x (any entry, or a ticked checkbox, counts) where a day or half day was given; delete it to undo.

Print

Print / Save as PDF prints the solution on screen, titled Final Results: the calendar (two weeks per landscape page), the legend and the totals table. The setup controls, tabs, collapse buttons and Potential Issues are left out, and everything fits the page width.

Save session and the phone team link (website only)

On the website (not the downloaded file), Save online… under Your work saves the month on the server and gives a 6-digit PIN. Write it down; after that every change saves by itself and a green bar at the top shows the PIN. Open by PIN… brings it back on any computer. A session is deleted 90 days after it was last opened, and anyone with the PIN can open it.

When the schedule is final and approved, Export → Publish team link makes a short read-only link for your team's phones. Update team link keeps the same link.

Save / Open a saved month

Under Your work at the bottom of the screen, Save my month (.json) downloads the whole month — team, roster, commitments, PTO, pins, and all your solutions — as a .json file. Open a saved month… reads one back. This is how you carry work between sessions, keep a reusable team template, or share a month with a co-chief: send them the file, they open it and see the same tabs.

Autosave

The app also autosaves to the browser automatically. The JSON file is your durable, portable copy — the one that survives a new machine or a cleared browser.

Clear scenario

Wipes everything — setup, roster, commitments, PTO, pins, and the solved schedule — back to an empty app, after a confirmation prompt. Use it to start a fresh month from scratch. It also clears the autosave, so Save my month first if you might want this month back.

11Errors glossary — hard, block Solve

CodeWhat it meansHow to fix
COMMITMENT_ON_CALL A commitment or clinic half-day lands on a call or post-call day. Move the commitment, or clear it with your program admin.
PTO_ON_CALL PTO lands on a call or post-call day. Move the PTO to a non-call-adjacent day.
PIN_OUTSIDE_WINDOW A pin targets someone outside their service window. Remove or fix the pin, or correct their start/end dates.
NIGHT_PIN_PM_COMMITMENT Someone is pinned to night call but also has a PM commitment that day. Remove one of the two conflicting marks.
CONTRADICTORY_PINS Two pins conflict — e.g. off + must-work the same day, two people both pinned night or pager the same day, an off pin on a call/post-call day, a pager pin on a call day, a PM half-off pin plus a pager pin, or a day/night pin on a non-call day. Remove one of the conflicting pins.
SINGLE_DAY_PERSON_UNAVAILABLE The person named to work alone on a Force One Person Only day is on PTO or off service that day. Choose Solver chooses, or someone else.
FORCED_DAY_ON_CALL A Forced Staffing Day lands on a call day. Everyone works call, so it cannot be forced. Remove it from Forced Staffing Days.
FORCED_DAY_TOO_MANY_STUCK More people than the forced count have a clinic or a Must Work / Pager pin that day. Remove a pin or clinic, force two instead of one, or remove the forced day.
OFF_ON_COMMITMENT An off pin lands on a day that person has a commitment. Remove the pin or the commitment.
HALFOFF_ON_COMMITMENT A half-day off pin overlaps a commitment in the same half. Remove one of them.
ATTENDING_DAY_OUTSIDE_MONTH The attending is set to cover the pager on a date outside the month. Take that day back from the attending.
ATTENDING_DAY_INVALID The attending is set to cover the pager on a call or post-call day. Take that day back from the attending.
NIGHT_ALTERNATION_IMPOSSIBLE With two seniors and one intern, the intern must take every other call night, and your pins make that impossible. Change the night or day-call pins.
CARRYIN_REQUIRED The anchor is post-call but carry-in wasn't filled in. Fill in carry-in (see §3).
DEGENERATE_COMPOSITION A call day's on-service roster can't staff call — e.g. no senior on that day, or fewer than two people on service. (A Medicine C team of two or more seniors is allowed without an intern.) Adjust service windows or the roster.
QUOTA_IMPOSSIBLE Someone has fewer eligible days than their off quota — every other day is call, post-call, PTO, or a commitment. Days off are a hard requirement, so this can never be solved around. Free up a day (move a clinic), or lower the off quota.

W_CONSEC_NIGHT_SLACKSomeone takes night call on back-to-back call days — only when no other arrangement exists.
W_DIDACTICS_HALF_OFFA half day off lands on someone's own didactics half — that session is missed.
W_CARRYOUTWhoever takes the last night of the month is post-call on the 1st of next month — tell next month's chief.
After an app update

If the app has been updated since you last solved a month, opening that scenario shows a note above the calendar: "This schedule was built under older scheduling rules." Press Solve and it rebuilds the month under the current rules — your roster, clinics, PTO, pins and attending days are all kept. You do not need to clear the scenario or re-enter anything. A schedule solved by the current version keeps re-solving stably, exactly as before.

If you ever see this

The app also folds any independent-audit hard-rule violation into this same panel. If you ever see a code starting with A_ here, the solver and the auditor disagree with each other — treat it as a bug and report it. (A_FORCED_STAFFING means a Forced Staffing Day does not have exactly the forced number working. A_CONSECUTIVE_NIGHTS can appear when no other arrangement exists and does not block export.)

13Report a problem

If a schedule looks wrong, the app says something confusing, or you have an idea, press the amber Report a problem button at the top of the screen. You can also use the Send this month to us links that appear next to a "No feasible schedule" message, under Potential Issues, and when the checker finds a broken rule.

What gets sent — and only this, only when you press Send:

  • your message,
  • the app version, and
  • your month (setup, roster, pins and your solutions) if the box "Attach my schedule (recommended)" is ticked. It is ticked by default because it lets us see exactly what you see. Untick it to send just your message.

Nothing else is sent anywhere, and nothing is sent unless you press Send.

14Get the app

The Download button at the top gets you the newest version so you can run it offline on your own computer — no install, no account, nothing uploaded. The downloaded file sends nothing anywhere unless you use Report a problem. (The website itself counts anonymous page visits; the downloaded file does not.)

  • Download the app (one HTML file) — the whole app as a single file. Save it anywhere and double-click to open it in your browser. You always get the newest version.
  • Starter kit (.zip) — the app plus this guide and a few example months, with a one-click "Click me" launcher.

The app never checks for updates on its own. To see whether you are current, compare the version next to the title (click it to see what's new) with the latest release linked from the Download window. If you opened the downloaded file, the window shows which version you have.

16Tips & troubleshooting

  • Set Month and Anchor first — a wrong anchor shifts every call day in the month.
  • Fix all Errors before Solve will do anything.
  • Use Load the example month to see a complete, working month before building your own. The empty page offers two: a 3-person team all month, and a 3-person team that becomes a 2-person team on the 16th.
  • Iterate with freeze so approved weeks stay put while you tweak the rest.
  • Colors on screen = colors in the xlsx = colors when you print. One language, everywhere.
  • Everything is local: no data leaves your machine unless you press Send on a problem report. The file from Save my month is your save file.
